Why it can be trusted

  1. The proof steps appear here after a read.

The whole verifier is one file. It fetches the head files, the snapshot's manifest and a few dozen 4 KB pages, and refuses the answer if any signature or hash fails. A page byte that is flipped in transit is caught by the trie's keccak hashes; a flipped head byte, by the committee's signature or a Merkle branch. The first read also fetches each layer's hints, about 1.4 MB, so that later reads skip the layers that cannot hold their keys; a hint that lies costs a round, never an answer. Later reads reuse the index pages, the account trie's top levels and the sync committees the earlier ones checked.

Left out, compared with the full version: checkpoint rotation, the by-hash table, the finalized tier and the visualization. None of them is needed for correctness.
